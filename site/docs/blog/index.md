    [current documentation](../index.md) for the maintained release.

Each post includes its original title (an empty `original_title` for untitled posts), publication and update timestamps, author, Blogger ID, and original URL in YAML front matter. Untitled posts use their publication date as the displayed heading.

The bodies retain Blogger's HTML, including formatting, tables, and links. Relative links are resolved against the original post URL. The available embedded image is copied to `assets/round-12-h.jpg`; the chart at `http://xdelta.org/2005-12month.png` no longer returns an image and its original reference is retained. Other historical external links may also be unavailable. Reader comments and Blogger page chrome are not included.
